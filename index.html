01 — About

Learning and operations depth, with a data analyst's mindsetProfessional summary

I'm an operations, quality and capability-development professional with over a decade of experience supporting Vodafone UK and Ireland operations. My work sits where process meets data: high-volume quality audits, reconciliations, KPI and MIS reporting, service-level monitoring, escalation management, UAT and operational readiness.

I build decision-ready reporting and process improvements using Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Excel and SQL, and I bring strong transferable capability for Vendor, Customer, Material and Finance master data operations — with a focus on data accuracy, governance, customer experience and continuous improvement.
